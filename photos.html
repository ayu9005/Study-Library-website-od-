Refund and Cancellation Policy

Refund Policy

Libraries usually do not have refund policies for borrowed items. Late fees might apply for overdue returns, but once a book is checked out, it's not common to receive a refund. It's recommended to adhere to due dates and contact the library for specific cases or concerns.

Please contact our customer support team at shribalajilibrary87@gmail.com in case of need any help.

Cancellation Policy

Library book cancellation policies vary, but in general, libraries typically do not have a cancellation policy for borrowed books. Instead, they often have due dates by which the borrowed items must be returned. If you need to cancel a hold or reservation, it's advisable to contact your library directly for specific information.
